A row per person, a column per database, and a chip saying which role they hold on
            each. The two axes are separate facts — somebody can be an analyst on one database and a viewer

Merge requests

          
When a member fills in missing data, the change lands in their own copy — not in the
            real database. It waits here for you. Your own edits never appear on this screen, because they
            were saved the moment you made them.

          

          

            Nothing waiting. A queue should be visibly empty when it is.
